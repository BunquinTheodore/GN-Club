# GN Club Marketing Site — Premium Design Audit

**Prepared for:** GN Club
**Scope:** Home, Work (index + case study details), Services, About, Contact — reviewed via screenshots and source code across four independent design-director passes.

---

## Executive Summary

The site has a strong visual system (dark UI, glass panels, duotone photography, lime accent) but is not yet safe to launch publicly in its current state: it ships **fabricated and literal placeholder content as if it were finished** — fake hero stats presented as real facts, a case study whose copy is unreplaced copywriter instructions ("PLACEHOLDER — describe what the sponsor needed..."), and stat fields reading "TBD." That is the single most damaging category of finding, because it isn't a polish issue, it's a trust issue for prospects evaluating a production company on precision and reliability.

The second major theme, cutting across nearly every page (Home, Work, Services, About, every CTASection, the Footer), is a **structural robustness gap in the scroll-reveal animation system**: content is mounted at `opacity: 0` and depends entirely on a live `whileInView` IntersectionObserver firing before it becomes visible, with no CSS/print/no-JS fallback. In full-page captures this reliably produces large blank black voids where core sales content (service tiles, case studies, the team, the CTA) should be — the opposite of "premium and finished." This single root cause (in `components/Reveal.tsx`, `PanelReveal.tsx`, and their per-page usages) should be fixed once, centrally, rather than patched page by page.

Beyond those two systemic issues, the remaining findings are page-specific polish and content items: image loading/priority gaps on the Work grid, contrast and consistency issues in the Contact form, a stray stock photo with baked-in unrelated text on every page's closing CTA, and small copy/data mismatches (a "six disciplines" headline over an 8-tile grid, an unbalanced case-study stat, a catch-all "Others" service tile).

---

## Findings by Page

### Home (`/`)

- **[P0] Fabricated hero stats presented as real data** — `lib/stats.ts` explicitly comments that the four hero stats ("150+ Events produced," "40+ Brand partners," "3 Countries covered," "12 Years running") are placeholder numbers, yet they render as animated, specific, count-up "facts" directly under the headline. **Why it matters:** this is the first concrete credibility claim a prospect sees; a wrong figure discovered later (e.g., in a sales call) is trust-breaking for an events company pitching enterprise/Web3 clients. **Fix:** get GN Club's real figures and replace the four values in `lib/stats.ts` before public launch, or substitute defensible qualitative proof (named flagship clients, a founding year from the About copy) until real numbers are confirmed. *(File: `lib/stats.ts`)*

- **[P1] "Six disciplines" headline contradicts the 8-tile service grid** — The section headline reads "Six disciplines, one production team, zero handoffs," but `lib/services.ts` lists 8 services and `ServiceBento` renders all 8 tiles. **Why it matters:** a visitor can literally count the tiles and see a mismatch — a careless-looking error on a page whose pitch is production precision. **Fix:** update the headline to "Eight disciplines..." or drop the count (e.g., "One production team, zero handoffs"), or consolidate the catalog to six groupings if that's the intended structure; keep copy and array length in sync going forward. *(File: `app/page.tsx`)*

- **[P1] Client logo trust bar is double-dimmed and partly missing real marks** — `Hero.tsx` wraps the "trusted by" strip in `opacity-70`, and `ClientLogos.tsx`'s own carousel container adds another `opacity-80` (net ~0.56 opacity). Three of five entries (Chainlink, Founders Summit, Studio One) have no logo asset in `lib/clients.ts` and fall back to low-contrast `text-fog-dim` text, which then gets doubly dimmed on top. **Why it matters:** a logo bar exists to borrow credibility from recognizable names at a glance; compounding dimming on the entries that most need visual weight (a real brand like Chainlink) defeats its purpose. **Fix:** remove one of the two opacity layers (keep only the wrapper's `opacity-70` or the carousel's `opacity-80`, not both), and source real logo marks for Chainlink, Founders Summit, and Studio One via `lib/media.ts` so all five render as marks, not a mix of images and dim text. *(File: `components/ClientLogos.tsx`)*

- **[P2] Below-the-hero content is invisible without a live scroll trigger** — Every section past the hero (services intro/`ServiceBento`, About row, Recent work grid, testimonials, CTA) mounts with Framer Motion `initial={{opacity:0}}` + `whileInView`, and stat values sit at their pre-animation `0`/`0+` state until their own observer fires, with no CSS-only or reduced-motion-independent fallback. **Why it matters:** anything that renders the page without replaying a live scroll (print/PDF export, an OG-image/thumbnail generator, a slow/janky mobile scroll, a full-page capture) shows most of the homepage as blank space with "0+" stats — exactly what full-page screenshots show today. **Fix:** add a `@media print` (and reduced-motion-safe) override forcing `opacity:1; transform:none` on Reveal/PanelReveal/CountUpValue elements; widen `whileInView` margin so the first section below the hero doesn't need a deep scroll to appear; verify under headless full-page capture. *(File: `components/PanelReveal.tsx`; also affects `Reveal.tsx`, `CountUpValue.tsx`)*

- **[P3] "Others" service tile undersells real capabilities** — One of the 8 `ServiceBento` tiles is titled plainly "Others," covering a specific, sellable item set (Fabrication, Booth Fabrication, Permanent Structure Construction, Permits & LGU Coordination, Manpower Deployment, Speaker Booking). **Why it matters:** "Others" reads as an unfinished taxonomy next to purpose-named tiles like "Video Production," undercutting the confident, specific naming used everywhere else. **Fix:** rename the entry in `lib/services.ts` to something specific, e.g. "Fabrication & Build" or "Site & Permits." *(File: `lib/services.ts`)*

### Work — index (`/work`)

- **[P0] Pickleball Edition case study ships literal placeholder copy** — The description reads "PLACEHOLDER — GN Club ran a community pickleball activation... Replace this description with the real client/brief details," the challenge/approach fields are unreplaced copywriter instructions, and two of three result stats show "TBD" (Players: TBD, Format: TBD). Visible in both the Work grid dialog and `/work/pickleball-edition`. **Why it matters:** this is a portfolio piece meant to prove production quality; visitors instead see internal authoring notes — the single most damaging content issue on the site. **Fix:** replace with real copy/stats before public launch, or temporarily remove the pickleball-edition entry from `lib/portfolio.ts` until it's written rather than leaving TBD/PLACEHOLDER live. *(File: `lib/portfolio.ts`)*

- **[P0] Flagship and other portfolio tiles render as solid black cards** — WOCEE 2026 (the flagship, largest row-span-2 tile), Pickleball, and Join Mazal render as flat black cards with only tag/title text visible, no photo, while other tiles load correctly. `DuotoneImage.tsx` never passes a `priority` prop and has no placeholder/blur fallback, so these `next/image` elements lazy-load and can paint as an empty `bg-ink` box until decoded. **Why it matters:** the largest, flagship tile on the page looking like a broken/missing image reads as unfinished production on a site whose whole pitch is premium production values. **Fix:** pass `priority` (and appropriate `sizes`) to first-row/above-the-fold grid images in `PortfolioGrid`, especially the WOCEE tile, and add a placeholder (`blurDataURL` or a distinguishable low-contrast skeleton) to `DuotoneImage` so unloaded tiles read as "loading," not "empty." *(File: `components/PortfolioGrid.tsx`; also `components/DuotoneImage.tsx`)*

- **[P1] Grid leaves large dead zones below the first row** — Only 5 of 9 defined case studies (`lib/portfolio.ts`) are visible in a full-page capture, followed by roughly 900px of empty black space before the CTA band; cards use `whileInView` with `initial={{opacity:0}}` and no non-JS fallback, so cards whose observer hasn't fired are fully invisible while still occupying their grid track. **Why it matters:** 4 of 9 case studies have no guaranteed-visible baseline — any delay in the observer firing (slow JS, remote screenshot/crawl tooling, a fast anchor jump) leaves real sales content permanently blank with an orphaned gap. **Fix:** don't start cards at opacity 0 — use a safer resting state (e.g., 0.4–1, never fully invisible), or reduce the IntersectionObserver margin so cards resolve as soon as they're laid out. *(File: `components/PortfolioGrid.tsx`; shared root cause with the Services grid below)*

- **[P2] No filtering despite an existing tag taxonomy** — Each case study carries a distinct `tag` (Consumer Electronics Expo, Community Activation, Conference, Web3 Activation, Product Launch, Party, etc.) in `lib/portfolio.ts`, but `PortfolioGrid` renders all 9 as one undifferentiated grid. **Why it matters:** a flat grid of 9 visually different engagement types reads like a dumped media library rather than a curated, confident portfolio. **Fix:** add a lightweight filter-pill row above the grid sourced from the existing unique `tag` values (glass-panel styling, lime active state), filtering client-side — no new data model needed. *(File: `components/PortfolioGrid.tsx`)*

### Work — Case Study Detail (WOCEE 2026)

- **[P1] CTA backdrop photo has unrelated text baked into the image** — The stock Unsplash photo used for `contact.backdrop` (`lib/media.ts`, a generic concert-crowd shot) has text burned into the image itself reading "ALL I AM IS YOURS," clearly legible bottom-left and bottom-right at the site's final conversion moment, present on every page. **Why it matters:** random English text baked into an obviously non-GN-Club stock photo reads as a stray watermark or editing mistake right where the copy is trying to sound like an experienced production house. **Fix:** swap `contact.backdrop` for one of the site's real event photos already in the media map (e.g., `wocee.1`, `about.stage`, or `work.*`) through the existing `DuotoneImage` treatment. *(File: `lib/media.ts`)*

- **[P2] Third result-stat tile wraps to two lines, unbalancing the row** — `lib/portfolio.ts` sets the third stat's value to the full phrase "Fully branded build" instead of a short token like the other two ("Multi-day," "20+"). At `font-display text-3xl`, this wraps to two lines, making that `GlassPanel` tile visibly taller than its neighbors. **Why it matters:** the three-tile row is meant to read as a quick, punchy glance; a wrapped, taller tile breaks that rhythm and looks unfinished on an otherwise clean page. **Fix:** shorten to a stat-idiom value, e.g. `{ label: "Stage build", value: "Custom" }`, so all three values stay on one line at `sm:grid-cols-3`. *(File: `lib/portfolio.ts`)*

- **[P3] Hero backdrop signage text competes with foreground content** — The `DuotoneImage` backdrop behind the hero (opacity 0.5) has the event's own on-site signage ("THE NEXUS STAGE," "TECH TAL[K]") visible through the duotone treatment, overlapping the H1 and the three stat tiles. **Why it matters:** legible background text adds visual noise right behind the page's primary scannable elements. **Fix:** add a `heroOpacity` prop to `DuotoneImage` defaulting lower than the gallery-tile usage, or add a stronger bottom-to-top gradient scrim behind the text/stat-tile column. *(File: `components/CaseStudyPanels.tsx`)*

### Services (`/services`)

- **[P1] Bento grid leaves large dead zones below the first row** — Only 2 of 7 defined services are visible in a full-page capture, followed by ~900px of empty black space before the CTA band, for the same root cause as the Work grid: cards mount at `initial={{opacity:0, y:...}}` via `whileInView` with no non-JS fallback, occupying their grid track while invisible. **Why it matters:** 5 of 7 services — core sales content — have no guaranteed-visible baseline; slow JS, screenshot/crawl tooling, or a fast anchor jump leaves them permanently blank. **Fix:** same as the Work grid — use a safer resting opacity (0.4–1, never fully invisible) or reduce the IntersectionObserver margin so cards resolve on layout rather than deep scroll. *(File: `components/ServiceBento.tsx`)*

- **[P2] Flagship tile's bullet list is cramped and noisy** — The `lg` tile's 11-item bullet list (Various Activations, Flagship Tech Events, Trading Competitions... Roadshow Presentations) runs as a single dense column with near-zero (`py-px`) line spacing in a card only ~230px tall in full mode. **Why it matters:** it's the grid's flagship, highest-weight tile, but ends up the most visually noisy — a tall stack of tiny, tightly packed bullets versus the clean, breathing smaller tiles next to it. **Fix:** for the `lg` span, lay items out in two columns (`grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-4` instead of a single `ul`) so 11 items resolve to ~6 short rows, and bump spacing to `py-0.5`. *(File: `components/ServiceBento.tsx`)*

### About

- **[P0] Story/team sections and every CTA render as blank voids without a live scroll trigger** — The second `AlternatingRow` ("Built for brands whose communities show up online first"), the entire "Our story" `CompanyTimeline`, the entire "The team" `TeamGrid`, and the `CTASection` headline/subhead/button on every page all mount with `initial={{opacity:0, y:16-40, scale:0.97}}`, reaching visibility only via `whileInView`. In full-page screenshots these render as literal blank black voids — the About page has ~1400px of empty space where the timeline and team should be, and case-study CTA bands show only the bare backdrop photo with no headline, copy, or button. **Why it matters:** this is not a screenshot artifact but a real robustness gap — anyone reaching these sections via an in-page anchor jump, End/Page Down, a screen reader landmark jump, slow/blocked JS, a print, or automated capture/share-preview tooling sees genuinely empty page real estate on a site whose current pass is specifically about looking premium and finished. **Fix:** don't gate primary content's visibility purely on IntersectionObserver firing before paint — add a `<noscript>`/CSS fallback forcing `opacity:1; transform:none` on reveal-tagged elements (mirroring the existing `prefers-reduced-motion` handling), and loosen `viewport={{ once: true, margin: '-10% 0px' }}` (`Reveal.tsx` L26, `PanelReveal.tsx` L43-44) to a small positive margin or `amount: 0` so elements register the instant any pixel is on-screen instead of after a further 10-15% scroll. **This is the same root cause as the Home/Work/Services reveal findings above and should be fixed once, centrally.** *(File: `components/Reveal.tsx`; also `components/PanelReveal.tsx`, `components/CTASection.tsx`)*

- **[P2] Hero copy asks for trust before offering any proof** — The hero subhead ("GN Club creates high-impact event activations for tech and Web3 brands in the Philippines and globally. If you need an event that moves your community, let's talk.") names the niche and pivots straight to a soft CTA without stating scale or proof (years running, notable clients/venues, event count), even though the page's own `CompanyTimeline`/`TeamGrid` further down implies that proof exists. **Why it matters:** the first screen of an About page is prime real estate for one concrete credibility signal; asking to "talk" before any evidence wastes it. **Fix:** add one concrete clause before the CTA sentence, e.g. "...from 350-person conferences to rooftop product drops" — this phrase already exists lower on the page (`app/about/page.tsx` L47) and just needs to be surfaced higher, where it does more persuasive work. *(File: `app/about/page.tsx`)*

### Contact

- **[P1] Form fields have no stable background, riding on a busy photo behind the glass panel** — Every input sits on `bg-white/5` inside a `.glass-panel` that is itself only `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` over a blurred concert photo (`globals.css` `--glass`). The photo's bright stage lights and a hand silhouette show through directly behind placeholder text in the "Full name," "Email," and message fields. **Why it matters:** a form is the one place legibility can't be optional; contrast riding on whatever happens to be behind that spot in the photo reads as unpolished, the opposite of the calm, confident dark-UI tone elsewhere. **Fix:** give the fields themselves (not just the outer panel) a real, more opaque surface, e.g. swap `fieldClassName`'s `bg-white/5` for `bg-ink-raised/70`, independent of the hover/focus gradient-ring treatment. *(File: `components/ContactForm.tsx`)*

- **[P1] Single contact channel with no visible reassurance copy** — The only contact info on the page is one Gmail address (repeated in the below-the-fold footer); there's no phone/WhatsApp, no office/city, and no visible response-time promise — the "expect a reply within one business day" line exists only inside the post-submit success toast (`ContactForm.tsx` L57), invisible until after submission. **Why it matters:** a form asking for name, email, phone, company, service type, and message is a real commitment; without visible reassurance about what happens next, that ask feels riskier, and a bare Gmail address undercuts credibility for a company pitching large-scale global activations. **Fix:** add a short reassurance line near the form itself (not just the toast), e.g. under the submit button: "We reply personally within one business day." Consider surfacing `site.socials` (Facebook/Instagram/X) as a secondary contact path directly on this page instead of only in the footer. *(File: `app/contact/page.tsx`)*

- **[P2] Optional-field labeling is inconsistent (Phone vs. Company)** — The Phone input has no `required` attribute and its placeholder says "Phone (optional)"; the Company input is equally unrequired but its placeholder just says "Company / brand" with no optional marker. **Why it matters:** a visitor scanning the form has no reliable way to tell which fields are mandatory; the inconsistency implies Company is required when it isn't, risking hesitation or unnecessary friction. **Fix:** either add "(optional)" to the Company placeholder to match Phone, or drop "(optional)" from Phone and mark the truly required fields (name, email, service, message) with a consistent asterisk/label convention instead. *(File: `components/ContactForm.tsx`)*

- **[P2] Large empty band below the form with no supporting content** — The page is only eyebrow + heading + one-line email + the form panel, wrapped in a section with `py-40` (10rem top and bottom), producing a large empty black band between the form and the next content, with no trust signal, FAQ teaser, or next-steps content. **Why it matters:** a contact page is a conversion moment at the point of highest intent; competitors typically pair the form with light trust reinforcement there — here the page reads as thin/unfinished rather than intentionally minimal. **Fix:** tighten section padding (e.g., `py-28`), and/or add a compact supporting element below the panel reusing an existing component — a condensed `ClientLogos` strip, or a 3-step "Discovery call → Proposal → Production" mini-timeline. *(File: `app/contact/page.tsx`)*

- **[P3] Message field has no visual priority over other inputs** — The select ("What do you need help with?") and the message textarea stretch full-width at the same height/style as the two-column name/email/phone/company row; the select's chevron sits far right with a lot of empty space next to short labels once a value is picked, and nothing marks the message field as the form's primary input. **Why it matters:** giving every field identical visual weight flattens hierarchy — the message textarea (the actual brief) should read as the anchor of the form, not just one of six equal boxes. **Fix:** increase the textarea's `rows` (e.g., to 6) or add top margin before it to separate it from the paired rows above, signaling it as the primary field. *(File: `components/ContactForm.tsx`)*

- **[P3] Footer contact/social info depends entirely on scroll-triggered reveal** — Every block in `Footer.tsx` (email, social links, copyright) is wrapped in `Reveal`, starting at `opacity:0` and animating in only via `whileInView` with a `-10%` viewport margin, with no non-JS/immediately-visible fallback. **Why it matters:** the footer is the site's only listing of phone-free alternative contact channels — on the Contact page specifically, where content above is otherwise thin, gating the sole secondary contact info behind an intersection-observer animation is an avoidable robustness risk (slow hydration, reduced-motion edge cases, automated capture tooling). **Fix:** drop the `Reveal` wrapper from the footer's contact/social column (or reduce it to a very short/no-op fade) so this information is guaranteed visible immediately. *(File: `components/Footer.tsx`)*
